Balancing chemical equations

Step 1: Write reactants and products

C3H8 + O2 → CO2 + H2O

Step 2: Find one atom that occurs only in one substance on both sides

C3H8 + O2 → CO2 + H2O

Step 3: Find coefficients to balance this atom

1C3H8 + O2 → 3CO2 + H2O

Step 4: Find another unbalanced atom which occurs in only one substance

1C3H8 + O2 → 3CO2 + H2O
